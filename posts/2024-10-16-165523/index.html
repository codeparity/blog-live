Miss Veronica vs Mrs (Dingle) Berry
2 souls apart - fighting each other.

Miss veronica : is an entity which was born when veronica was around 16.
Veronica-ness : is about grace, beauty and lots of admirers

That was few 35 years ago.

Time has passed, kids were born, marriage happened - but veronica lived.

Veronica is what makes gVeronica gets up, put on new clothes, keep a account of which ‘dress’ was worn when...
who has seen veronica in that ‘dress’...
repeats
and hair
and saloon
and workouts
shopping ... lots
and then there is all the page 3 stuff - the thing which makes veronica do all this...

and finally - job.

Jobs are exciting for veronicas.
Simpies are everywhere, and so are the sigmas.

Sigmas are a bet - sometimes they work
sometimes they come between daily veronica things.

Mrs Veronica Dingle Berry is ‘smart' version of veronica.
She thinks about money.
less about ‘now’, more about ‘future’.

And what can go wrong when the sigma is handsome (ppl say so), has a tesla, a big home, kewl sigma life and a title - historical consistency.

3 years - they flew. The alcohol was modern, the parties were super modern and this is how sigma business is done.

She woke up.

It seems like a haunted dream. Everything has gone wrong...There were so many ‘frens’ who told her ‘to choose'...and she did.

But veronica feels let down - by all. It was like the whole world wanted her to not become global veronica.
to add insult to the injury - mr dingle berry is going down - to never come up - her logical mind knows that.

She is stuck - blaming her luck.
She made a ‘bet’ - a very well informed 1, but seems like the devil tricked her - using her hacks.

She is missing ‘something’...its not amit/ashish...
it is the ‘time’....when she loved herself...

mrs dingleberry and miss veronica - a love which is lost in time....
