season 1 flashback

veronica is a happy go lucky girl.
a good pretty protected life....

fathers and figures with an innocent soul...shielded her from most battles of life.
she has a spark, a twinkle in her eyes..

college is over, the 1st love sweetheart is on his career journey.
money comes into picture, things dont work out...

the charm and spark are met with capitalism, perception.

being let down by a lower order sigma, who was just a cute popular chocolate boi, created a scar.

being brave and proud, she takes on professional life to reclaim her identity, her worth.

father figures spot her - for the spart and fire is unmistakable.

she is successful, yet the scar is there, hidden beyond all the wins.

She gives up faith in her choices...marries a familia recommended man, a honest hardworking 1, as flawed as any man is.

has it all, yet the hunger and right to earn her primeness drives her.

motherhood - she is blessed...she loves the kid, but also knows her duties : her daughter wont repeat the same mistakes, and suffer from them.

for her, feminism has bigger meaning - the patriarchy of hindu/bengali society has denied her choices, her righful place.

7 years of marriage - and reality becomes too stark - he friends and their page 3/facebook life - trips, wins and glamour...
when she knows she is PRIME.

Fate gives her her opportunity, a righful 1
a sigma sensei has recalled her spark, given her a chance of lifetime - make different choices.

and here he was : all charming, experienced, aware and proven winner
