....is a Hindi proverb that means a person who is ashamed or frustrated by their own failure or mistake will express their anger by quarreling with others or doing something unproductive.

The phrase literally translates to "a shame-faced cat scratching a pillar" and originates from the behavior of a cat that, after failing to catch its prey, scratches a nearby post out of frustration.

Meaning: It describes someone who is embarrassed about their own shortcomings and vents their anger by lashing out irrationally or making baseless accusations.

Origin: The idiom is based on the natural behavior of cats. When a cat is unsuccessful in hunting, it may scratch at a pillar or wall in a fit of frustration.
