I dunk a lot on Dubai

i just hate the city and thought behind it

  • the peeps i worked with there - quite amazing.

It was something - this assignment in the desert.

Driving to Sharjah for work, and coming back in wee hours in evening.

There was nothing to complain though .... i dint like the life which this city offered after work.

Dynamic pricing of hotels is not a new thing ....

was unable to get a hotel stay extended - due to some 'official confusion'.

so had to find a hotel in evening - landed in a shady 1, which just had a single room available - 1st floor

I took it, coz i just needed a bed for few hours - the day starts again.

but this was 'special'. The hotel had a 'dance bar' at the ground floor.

It became apparent why i got this room...

sleeping was impossible, the bed shaked with every cheap beat.

Tried getting a different room - but all in vain - nothing till 'tomo'..

so i went out...chatting with a betti/veronica thing...at 1 am...

the conversation - was even more triggering than the beats of dance bar.

So just went further out...maybe to eat something, or mostly smokey...

It is quite a wonderful city - after the dark...

security is not really a problem, 1 can walk around as much as 1 wants...provided he is sober.

Dubai - was transforming from a smoker friendly country...to a west approved 1...

these were the last years of it.

smokes were cheap, and it was like everyone smoked.

Found a decent grocery store...bought a pack of dunhill something...

and took a moment to see the city in its silence...

it wasnt lit like a mall, but it seemed at ease

I wasnt noticing much around...for this was my 2 or 3 stay there.

it felt like a second home to me...for silence was there.

Someone asked for a light. A woman....a very attractive 1 - alone.

she must be having an awful night too...for a woman like her...at this hour in this city...it was impossible.

anyways, i dint have any reasons to think further...coz laws in dubai are kinda ...subjective.

But she dint really went away... she asked if i had malboro, the dunhill pack which she had....

maybe it wasnt hers...

I offered mine- coz all these 'flavors' seemed rather like labels to me

  • for i was a desi klassic milds loyalist..(whose nicotine levels...were clearly not marked for a reason :))

She refused.

it kinda felt offending, to leave or move..

so i stayed where i was...

and this woman, seemed to be ok with it.

Smokeys became the conversation starter...

and boi - the woman knew her smokes.

She traveled around....maybe a few years younger or older - i could not tell or ask.

i told her about my hotel and my current 'why' of being there...

and she told her 'why's

That was me meeting Veronica Prime...

We did meet ... for few days...

but she was busy...and i was too - in those days.

...we kept in touch via mails....

a mail a year thing .... to absurd to share with 'anyone'...

and too infrequent to label it.

we both grew...now 15+ years of it....just mails.

she dint tell me her real name .... she confessed after few 10 years...

but i kind of expected it...considering the circumstances of our 'meet'.

Recently, the 'fren' discovered this bleh blah that i do...

and it did help her....for confessions and truths - are hard to come for a Veronica..

and this was Global Veronica Prime for me...

too different....yet fren in the 'invisible'
