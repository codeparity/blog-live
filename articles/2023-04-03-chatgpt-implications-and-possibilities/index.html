There is a lot of "apprehensions" regarding ChatGPT and its consequences on careers and humanity.

Fears are further instigated when countries like China, Italy etc ban it.

Current TechGod / "Iron Man" also warned about it!!!

It is true - a lot of us and our friends would lose their jobs. Gradually or suddenly is just a matter of Geo-economics, time and their relative situation in their professional life.

It is not gloom and doom for humanity as I see it.

I ll explain by sharing my “opinions” regarding what ChatGPT really is.

Functional implications

Calculators, computers, supercomputers, distributed computing, and now cloud clusters – all changed society.

Technology is, and has been a tool, which offers efficiency, convenience and reliability.

ChatGPT and other competing generative AI are just large datasets - which generate on a subset of human thought, sacrificing accuracy.

To put it bluntly - It is a tool to aid "creativity" of our work process. Think decks and their most impressive implementation, without much cursor draggaing and resizing. Improved efficiency.

For coders, it essentially means how accurately they can describe "constraints" over a known piece of software framework - whether its kernel, middle-ware or UI/UX. The intent - still has to come from a human.

"Intent" - the key.

Intent is the thought which comes before action, and no matter what latest TechGod tells you - Intent is technically what a programmer specifies to code to do.

This is and would be always true - we still compute on inorganic, difficult to make silicon based devices - logically dependent on the amount of data-centers, bandwidth, electricity, water and silicon world can offer.

Practical implications

Decisions, strategy, evolution - are still in our hands - that is what this era is about - evolving with times, in intelligence, in productivity – for individuals and small teams.

Technically, one can implement an idea without much domain expertise. Engineers, hence their "mangers" would increasingly find themselves at the receiving end of "unfortunately, we have to let you go" kind of communications.

Emotional Implications

"This wont affect me"
“I am too important..”
“I am an expert…”
“I am a leader with...”

Fear, Denial, Anger - the whole cycle is bound to happen.

I choose to save some time - skipped "Fear, Denial, Anger" to focus on problem solving. It is not easy, but becomes easy with time.

What now

Learn ChatGPT - this is an exciting time when domain knowledge disappears, and one can create exciting products without any knowledge/entry barriers.

I intend to use this shiny new "ratchet"/skill to create something unique, previously deemed unachievable by an independent programmer. I feel it is power for those who yield it, doom for those who fear it.

Tech, evolution, improvement seems inherent to our core human nature - validated umpteen times since industrial revolution, and further accelerated by globalization - there is no point fearing it.
