a leo veronica and $veronica ; the frens of not to be.

leona : noes she is supposed to ‘rule’...

she is built that way : gifted likey it...

and fights like it.

leona always need an empire to rule.

it is just the ‘purpose’ of her divine manufacturing.

bright as sun - made to inspire : cultivate ‘life’.

this all - is fine and dandy..

but here on earth : she meets her 3d familia..

proud of her abilities, prude of the pretty...

hungry for her light.

but she was born in kuk era..

what she expects : is absolute - honesty..

and honesty is what her beauty of 3d : is made to deny her...

world turns sunny : where she goes..

but she is told, it was always this way..

it is the lies : which the leo fem : is made to accept in democraxy..

where her worth : is so equal : and is so - infuriating to her insides..

...the grace of a feline, which deserves the respect of leo.

in her wanderings and experiments, she meets a taurus the bull...

busy in his nature, happy in his lazy, unaware of the words which world writes about him..

wen she is angry and deserted, leona discovers the taurus, and the hurt of not being ‘respected’ for her ‘grace’..

becomes the happy which she so deserves..

she is made to shine..

and he is made to not - bother..

the ‘balance’ of this weird relationship : becomes the part she craves wen alone..

yet the lazy which she wants to hide..

she needs him in her jungle...for the wants of it.

this was fine - till 1971..

wen she was to be pretty and sun of a home..

but white boi - invented ‘feminism’...

and she was psy/oped into being a money leader as well..

the curse of shine : which all blackholes are made to devour..

took on a path : to dance to the muggles of imaginary money : for the jungle that is the urban dystopia..

..she plays, she wins, she gets confused..

she noes : she is happy with an empire..

but still needs a ‘cave’...

a cave which only a wolfie has..

but that wolf : has to be a taurus ; for this weird balance of hers..

she needs to shine..she was made for it..

but it is not in her nature : to parade her grace for few shmekles..

...it is so unnatural for the leona, to do this for money : for kudos of lesser men ....

thinking : is so against her nature..

yet wen she gets old...the jungle looks like more of a zoo to her..

she wants to retire to her cave : where animals and nature disappear..

yet her cave : is neon led lights with no peace : which she has to live as a home.

leona or cat : is just ‘choice’ of her...

for she plays cat : for the people she respects : the religion : the elderlies and the parents..

the page 3 metaverse : which she so powers with her radiance.

all of whom : used her to shine to power their lives..

is the truth : leona cant accept...

coz she is too proud..to not hunt..

yet she cant : for the words which got her - tamed.

it is not like : she ll ever give up..

for hope is her inbuilt function...

but the quantification is ‘money’ : which always comes at an elderlie grace for ‘time’.
