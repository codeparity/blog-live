The “DishBrain” Experiment: Biological Gaming

In 2022, a startup called Cortical Labs achieved a world first: they taught 800,000 living brain cells in a dish to play the video game Pong. This is the literal “Gamepad + Brain” correlation.

  • How it works: Scientists grew human and mouse neurons on a microelectrode array (a silicon chip that can “talk” to cells). The electrodes sent electrical pulses to the neurons to tell them where the ball was.
  • The “Gamepad” Interface: The neurons acted as the “paddle.” When they fired in a certain pattern, the paddle moved.
  • The Learning Loop: If the neurons “hit” the ball, they received a predictable, organized electrical pulse (a reward). If they “missed,” they were hit with a random, chaotic pulse (punishment).
  • Result: Within five minutes, the biological “wetware” learned to play the game—faster than many digital AIs do in their early training phases.

🎙️ Brainoware: The Biocomputer Evolution

  • Voice Recognition: In experiments, Brainoware was trained to recognize human voices. It reached 78% accuracy in distinguishing between eight different speakers just by “listening” to the electrical patterns of sound.
